# Brag Plan: Mera Khet

## What is this app?
Mera Khet is a farm-membership platform in Sujangarh, Rajasthan — you reserve 1, 3, or 6 wheat plots for a season, the founder's team farms it for you (soil-tested, tracked via CCTV and updates), and the harvest comes home to you: delivered, milled into flour, or sold.

## The angle
Not a SaaS demo — a real farm, a real person's plot, watched all season and brought home. The video should feel like a quiet, premium farm-to-table film: warm light, real photography, a real member dashboard, and the one claim that isn't marketing copy — that part of every plot goes to feed families who need it.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Full-bleed real photo (a farmer's hand brushing through ripening wheat at golden hour) with the site's actual headline typed on in the display serif: "Your own wheat, grown for you." Warm cream-to-ink gradient at the base for legibility.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The Member Dashboard preview card from the hero — "3 Plots · 21,780 sq ft · Gehu (Wheat) · Upcoming" — this is a real member's plot, not a mockup pitch.
- The Farm Camera live viewer: the pulsing "DEMO CAMERA" indicator and the camera tab switcher (Main Field → Farm Entrance → Crop Area), over real farm CCTV stills — this is the site's most distinctive, least generic UI moment.
- The Feeding Families stat: "₹1,000 from every plot" with the contributed-amount and families-fed counters ticking up — the single most impressive, non-generic claim on the site.

## Outro / punchline
Cross-fade from the Feeding Families numbers into the wordmark "Mera Khet" on deep green, gold wheat icon, tagline "From our soil to your plate." Long hold. Silence at the very end (polished restraint).

## User flow worth showing
Entry → key action → result: reserve a plot on the pricing plans → watch your season on the Farm Camera / member dashboard (plot count, crop stage, live CCTV access) → harvest comes home (delivered, milled, or sold), with the Feeding Families impact riding along automatically. Scenes 2–3 show this flow directly (the dashboard card and the camera viewer), not just landing-page section recreations.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet, premium farm-to-table film — warm, honest, unhurried, rooted in real soil and real people, not corporate SaaS energy
- Interpretation: 4 scenes, longer holds (4-6s per scene), slow crossfades (0.6-0.8s), mixed-case light-to-medium type with generous letter-spacing, restraint over hype

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20s

## Share copy (draft)
Your own wheat plot in Rajasthan — farmed for you, watched by you all season, and it comes home to your table. Mera Khet is open for this season. 🌾

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed, restrained
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10-by-ende-dot-app.mp3 (109.96 BPM, 60s track, trimmed to ~20s) — the calmest/warmest of the bundled options, low volume posture
- Music treatment: start at 0s under the hook, gentle throughout, slight swell into the outro, fade out over the final ~1s (silence at the very end per polished restraint)
- Music cue guidance: preset exists (happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10). Strong cues in window: 20.19s and 20.74s (near the outro land) and beat grid every ~0.55s throughout — use beats near 9.29s/9.83s for the cut into the camera scene and near 15.28s/15.82s for the cut into the impact/outro scene; don't snap sequential text to every beat (too fast to read), just the scene-cut moments.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle — the live-pulse dot and stat counters may breathe slightly with the beat; nothing else reacts
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched, professional restraint — no more than 3-4 cues total
- Audio-coupled moments: hook headline settling in, camera-tab switch (soft click), stat counters ticking up, final wordmark landing
- Restraint rule: no dense rhythmic layer, no aggressive drops, nothing louder than the visuals; keep it a bed, not a lead

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook — 3s
Full-bleed hero photo (farmer-hand-wheat-sunset.jpg) with a warm gradient (cream→transparent at top, ink at the base). Headline settles in from a soft rise + fade: "Your own wheat," then "grown for you." on the next line (display serif, mixed case, generous letter-spacing). Small mono-data label above it: "SUJANGARH, RAJASTHAN".
Sequential/interaction: yes — the two headline lines settle in one beat apart (not typed character-by-character; a soft rise+fade, since polished tone avoids busy typing effects)
Audio intent: quiet, warm entrance; let the image and words breathe
Audio-coupled idea: a very soft rise/whoosh under the headline settle, nothing percussive
Music: warm bed begins, low volume
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Reveal (Member Dashboard) — 5s
Cross-fade to a recreation of the hero's "Member Dashboard" preview card, enlarged and centered on the cream background: "Member Dashboard · PREVIEW" label, "3 Plots" in display serif, "21,780 sq ft · ~0.5 acre", then a 2-column detail reveal: "Crop → Gehu (Wheat)" and "Status → Upcoming 🌾", each cell settling in a beat after the last. A small green "Farm Camera" pill with a video icon sits at the bottom of the card, foreshadowing Scene 3.
Sequential/interaction: yes — the two detail cells (Crop, Status) arrive one after the other, each with a soft settle
Audio intent: precise, confident — this is a real record, not a promise
Audio-coupled idea: a soft, single UI-arrival tick as each detail cell settles
Music: steady warm bed
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Highlight: Farm Camera — 6s
Cut to the dark "Farm Camera" viewer UI on the deep ink background (#232920): top bar reads "Farm Camera — Main Field" with a pulsing red "DEMO CAMERA" dot beside it (live-pulse ring animation, matched to the site's actual CSS keyframe), a real CCTV still filling the frame below. Midway through the scene, the camera tab switches from "Main Field" to "Farm Entrance" (simulated tap on the tab), swapping to the second CCTV still with a quick crossfade. Caption beneath, mono-data: "You don't have to take our word for it." settles in after the switch.
Sequential/interaction: yes — simulate a tap on the "Farm Entrance" tab partway through the scene, swapping the still and the active-tab highlight
Audio intent: alert but calm — the live-pulse dot should feel like a heartbeat, not an alarm
Audio-coupled idea: soft tap/click sound on the camera-tab switch; the pulse ring may pulse gently in time with the beat grid
Music: warm bed continues, slightly more present under the darker visual
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Impact + Outro — 6s
Cross-fade to the warm brown-soft background (#F1E4D3). Heart icon, then "Your plot feeds more than your family." settles in (display serif). Below it, two stat blocks count up together over ~1.5s: "₹[contributed]" / "Contributed so far" and "[families]" / "Families fed" (use the real formula: families = round(collected/1000 × 2); pick a representative in-season number, e.g. ₹1,84,000 contributed → 368 families fed, clearly a snapshot not a live claim). Hold briefly, then cross-fade the whole scene into the final card: deep green background (#263422), gold wheat icon, "Mera Khet" wordmark in display serif, tagline "From our soil to your plate." beneath it. Music fades out over the last ~1s; hold on the wordmark in near-silence.
Sequential/interaction: yes — the two stat numbers count up together (not staggered), then the scene cross-fades into the separate outro card
Audio intent: warm swell into pride, then quiet confidence at the very end
Audio-coupled idea: the count-up may tick softly in time with the beat grid; no sound on the final wordmark landing — let it hold in near-silence
Music: gentle swell approaching the outro card, then fades to silence over the last ~1s
Transition mood: soft crossfade → end

**Music mood for this video:** warm, restrained, mid-tempo bed (happy-beats vol-10, ~110 BPM) — never a lead, never a drop
**Audio summary:** A single continuous warm music bed under all four scenes, three sparse motion-matched SFX ticks (dashboard details, camera-tab switch, stat count-up), and a fade to near-silence under the final wordmark — restraint throughout, matching the polished/premium direction.
