**About:**
Mera Khet lets families reserve their own plot on our farm in Rajasthan for a full growing season. We grow the crop with soil-tested, responsible fertilizer use, and members can watch their plot 24×7 on live CCTV from sowing to harvest.

At harvest, members choose what happens next: raw grain delivered home, processed into flour, or sold at the market with the proceeds sent to them. Every plan also sets aside ₹1,000 per plot for our Feeding Families Fund, which donates wheat to families in need.

Mera Khet is run by MK Farms (Madanlal Krishnadevi Farms), a family farm going back to its roots.

Plans: 1 Plot, 3 Plots, or a full acre (6 Plots) — see merakhet.in.
