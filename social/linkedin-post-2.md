# LinkedIn Post #2 — Founder story (approved draft, post after next batch of connections)

My grandparents spent their whole lives on the land.
My generation spent ours in offices. 🌾

Somewhere between the two, we stopped knowing where our food comes from.
We buy atta in a packet, and never ask who grew the wheat, how, or on what soil.

So I started Mera Khet, named after my grandparents, as our way back to the farm.

The idea is simple:
→ You reserve your own plot on our family farm in Rajasthan
→ We grow wheat on it, with soil-tested, responsible care
→ You watch your plot grow on live CCTV, from sowing to harvest
→ Your harvest (250–300 kg per plot) comes home as grain or fresh flour

And ₹1,000 from every plot goes to our Feeding Families Fund, which gives wheat to families who need it.

Sowing starts near Diwali. Registrations close 31 October.

If you've ever wanted your family to eat wheat you can actually trace back to the soil, send me a message and I'll share the details. 🙏

What's one food you wish you knew the full story of? 👇
